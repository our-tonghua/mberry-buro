        Privacy Policy

        This notice explains how MBERRY BURO (Olha Malinko Design) handles personal data submitted through this website. The final text, controller details and legal basis must be confirmed by the client before launch.

        What we collect

        Your name, email, phone or messenger handle, property location, property type and size, the service format you select, your project description, and any plans, photos or links you choose to share.

        Why we use it

        Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal and, if you engage us, to deliver the agreed services. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

        How long we keep it

        Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 12 months. Project records are kept for as long as required by applicable law.

        Your rights

        Under the GDPR and other applicable laws you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to hello@mberryburo.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

        Analytics

        This website does not use analytics or marketing cookies. If analytics are added, they will only run after you give consent.
